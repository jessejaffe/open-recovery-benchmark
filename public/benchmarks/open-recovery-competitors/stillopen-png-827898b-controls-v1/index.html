Claim limitations

Named direct-repair products are compared only within the same frozen format cohort, using identical damaged inputs and healthy controls, complete terminal-outcome evidence, and independent PDF render/text or raster RGBA validation.

  • The source files are externally authored, but the structural mutations are benchmark-authored.
  • Do not compare products across formats or against cases outside their declared cohort.
  • Preview-only, watermarked, truncated, placeholder, unavailable, paywalled, or missing outputs do not pass.
  • Deleted-file and disk-recovery products require a separate storage-recovery protocol and are not scored here.
  • An unsigned attestation proves bundle integrity, not publisher identity.
