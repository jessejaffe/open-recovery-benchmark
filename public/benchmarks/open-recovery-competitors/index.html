What this shows

StillOpen led the registered PDF and PNG cohorts and tied ToolTea on JPEG. The broader product distinction is coverage: StillOpen provides one local browser workflow across all three tested formats; the PDF products are PDF-only, while ToolTea is image-only.

Why the longer market list is not scored here

Exclusion is documented market evidence, not a zero recovery score. Products without a complete comparable output cannot be honestly sent through the validator.

Download and install

Repairit, EaseUS Fixo, 4DDiG, Stellar desktop products, SysTools, and similar utilities create a materially different experience. Several trials also block saving.

Paywall, preview, or quota

Recovery Toolbox, OfficeRecovery, iLovePDF, Stellar Online, and several desktop trials do not provide enough unrestricted complete exports for this cohort.

Windows-only

Kernel tools, PDF Recovery Kit, and many Recovery Toolbox downloads are Windows applications rather than browser products available to the tested Mac user.

Different recovery task

Recoverit, R-Studio, and Recuva scan storage for deleted files. They require a separate disk-recovery corpus and denominator.

Open-source engines

qpdf, MuPDF, Ghostscript, pngfix, and WhatsApp JPEG Image Repair remain useful capability baselines, but require a terminal, installation or compilation, and manual file handling.

Claim boundary

This is not evidence about every corruption pattern or every file type. It is an auditable comparison on the frozen cases and versions identified here.
